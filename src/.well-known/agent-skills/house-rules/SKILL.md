# House rules and services

## Parking space

- Individual pitch for vans, caravans, motorhomes (up to over 6.5 m categories).
- Included: electricity, Wi-Fi, water access.
- Indoor shower/WC: on request, 7 h–21 h, when hosts are on site — 10 CHF / week.
- EV charging (slow): 15 CHF for 10 h, 30 CHF for a full day/night. Type 2 connector on a 220 V / 10 A socket (cable provided).
- Payment: credit card or cash (CHF or EUR).

## Eco studio

- 2 people in the bed; sofa bed for extra guests; infants 0–2 on request.
- Minimum stay 4 nights.
- Wi-Fi, private parking with EV charging, modern ecological equipment.
- No parties; respect the quiet village setting.

## General

- Location: La Sonnaz (Formangueires), Fribourg, Switzerland — quiet village edge with open-field views.
- Site languages: fr, en, de, es, pt, it, nl.
