# Getting to C&C

## Address

C&C — La Sonnaz (Formangueires), Canton of Fribourg, Switzerland.

## By car

- Less than 5 minutes from two A12 motorway exits: "Fribourg-Nord" and "Fribourg-Sud".
- Fribourg city and agglomeration (Villars-sur-Glâne, Givisiez, Granges-Paccots) within 10 minutes.
- Private on-site parking; EV charging available.

## By train / bike

- 1 km from Belfaux-Village station (connected to Fribourg).
- About 15 minutes by bike from Fribourg; ~20 minutes walking then train from the University of Fribourg (Unifr).

## Landmarks nearby

- Forum Fribourg (exhibition centre) and BCF Arena (Saint-Léonard ice rink) are close by.
- Ideal central base to visit all of Switzerland.
