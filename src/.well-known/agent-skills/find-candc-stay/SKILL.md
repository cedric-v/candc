# Find the right C&C stay

C&C offers two rentable units at the same location (La Sonnaz, Fribourg, Switzerland):

## Parking space (`parking-space`)

- Individual, equipped pitch for vans, caravans, and motorhomes (categories: standard_car, car_roof_tent, van, caravan, motorhome_upto_6_5m, motorhome_over_6_5m).
- 20 CHF / night + 3 CHF city tax per adult / night.
- Electricity, Wi-Fi, water, optional indoor shower/WC on request (10 CHF / week, 7 h–21 h).
- EV charging (slow): 15 CHF (10 h) or 30 CHF (full day/night), Type 2 connector on a 220 V / 10 A socket.
- Booking page: https://candc.ch/fr/parking/booking/

## Eco studio (`eco-studio`)

- Furnished ecological apartment for 2 people in the bed, plus a sofa bed (infants 0–2 on request).
- Minimum stay: 4 nights. Around 120–160 CHF / night depending on season.
- Wi-Fi, private parking with EV charging, quiet village setting.
- Booking page: https://candc.ch/fr/eco-studio/booking/

## How to choose

- Traveller sleeps in a vehicle → parking space.
- Traveller wants real accommodation (couple, remote work, longer stay) → eco studio.
- Group with both a van and a need for indoor lodging → propose booking both units separately.
